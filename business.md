# Lovely Bites

*Fictional business, made up for planning and testing. Not a real company.*

## Who we are

- Small independent event caterer based in St Saviour, Jersey, trading since 2014
- Run by a husband-and-wife team, a head chef and around 6 core staff, plus a pool of casual servers for busy weekends
- We cook from our own kitchen unit and buy local wherever we can: Jersey Royals, Jersey dairy, island-landed fish and shellfish, local growers for veg
- We cover the whole island, all 12 parishes

## Events we do

- **Weddings**: marquee, barn and hotel-venue receptions, from 40 to 250 guests. Our biggest line of work, May to September
- **Corporate**: finance and legal firm lunches, client evenings, staff parties and summer dos in St Helier
- **Private parties**: milestone birthdays, anniversaries and garden parties at home
- **Christmas parties**: busy November and December, mostly corporate
- **Wakes and memorials**: simple buffets at short notice
- **Community and charity events**: parish halls, fundraisers and sports club dinners

Serving styles: seated (plated or sharing), and buffet.

## Menu tiers

### Classic: honest, generous food
- Good for: buffets, wakes, office parties, informal parties
- Example: slow-roast pork shoulder or chicken with herbs, Jersey Royals, seasonal salads, homemade quiche, bread board, one pudding
- 2 courses (main + dessert), 2 choices for each course

### Signature: our most popular
- Good for: most weddings, client evenings, birthday dinners
- Example: island crab cakes or local goats' cheese tart, then rump of lamb or roast hake with Jersey Royals, then black butter tart or Jersey cream pannacotta
- 3 courses, 3 choices for each course, plus bread and butter

### Prestige: the full works
- Good for: high-end weddings and top corporate dinners
- Example: Grouville Bay oysters and canapé-style amuse-bouche, then local lobster or seared scallops, then fillet of beef, then a dessert plate, then Jersey cheeseboard and coffee with petit fours
- 4 courses plus amuse-bouche, with tailored menu planning and a tasting session included

Per-head prices, add-ons, hire, travel and food costs are in `menu-prices.csv`.

## Pricing rules

### Staffing
- **Servers**: 1 per 15 guests for seated events, 1 per 25 guests for buffets
- **Chefs**: 1 per 40 guests, whatever the serving style
- **Always round staff numbers up**. For example, 41 guests seated = 3 servers and 2 chefs
- **4-hour minimum** for every member of staff, even if the event is shorter

### Staff costs and premium hours
- Staff cost = Jersey minimum wage plus employer's social security, at the current rates
- **Time and a half** for every hour worked on a Jersey bank holiday
- **Time and a half** for every hour worked after 23:00

### Tax
- GST is added at the current Jersey rate (currently 5%) on the final total

### Deposit and validity
- **25% deposit** to confirm the booking
- **Quotes are valid for 30 days** from the date issued

### Price floor
- **Never quote below cost plus 25%**, where cost includes food, staff and any other direct costs
- If a requested discount would take the price below that floor, **we refuse the discount and explain why**, showing the floor price. We can offer something else instead, such as a cheaper menu tier or a buffet instead of seated service
